Opening academy

Three openings to explore

These are introductory move ideas, not a complete opening repertoire.

London System

1. d4 · 2. Bf4 · 3. e3

A flexible setup for White. Develop smoothly and support the center.

Italian Game

1. e4 e5 · 2. Nf3 Nc6 · 3. Bc4

Develop toward the center and put pressure near f7.

Sicilian Defense

1. e4 c5

An ambitious response to 1.e4 that fights for the center asymmetrically.

Queen's Gambit

1. d4 d5 · 2. c4

White challenges Black's central pawn and seeks active development.
